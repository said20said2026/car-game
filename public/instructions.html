Overcoming Road Hazards & Dynamic AI Traffic

The highway AI adapts dynamically to your speed and progression:

  • Yellow City Taxis: Cruise at moderate speeds but will abruptly signal and switch lanes. Keep a safe trailing distance.
  • Heavy Cargo Trucks: Move slowly and occupy a wide footprint. Never squeeze past them when road margins are tight.
  • Construction Pylons & Road Cones: Glancing collisions strip 30% of your instant kinetic speed. Steer clear to preserve engine momentum.
