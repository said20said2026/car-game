3. Local Device Storage (HTML5 LocalStorage)

Client-Side Game State Retention

City Speed Heroes operates on modern HTML5 LocalStorage technology to persist your in-game achievements, unlocked supercars, collected coins, personal best ghost laps, and garage upgrades. This data is stored entirely on your local device's browser sandbox and is never transmitted to or processed by any external cloud server. You retain full control to clear this data at any time via your browser's history and site data settings.

Family-Safe Sandbox Architecture

In full accordance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international youth protection standards, our platform does not require account creation, does not solicit real names, physical addresses, phone numbers, or social media links, and does not operate open chat rooms. City Speed Heroes provides a completely safe, wholesome, and non-exploitative gaming environment for youth and families.

Like most standard web servers, games-children.com generates anonymous aggregate server logs that may include IP addresses, browser types, internet service providers (ISPs), referring/exit pages, and timestamps.
