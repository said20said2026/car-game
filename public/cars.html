Vehicle Upgrade Systems

By collecting gold coins and pulling off high-risk Near Miss overtakes, you can visit the Garage to tune four essential mechanical components:

Top Speed Engine Tuning

Increases velocity ceiling on open highways to shatter previous track records.

Acceleration Boost

Reaches top speed in record time after braking or dodging obstacles.

Nitro Tank Capacity

Expands nitrous reserve capacity and flow rate for prolonged hyper-drive speed bursts.

Handling & Responsive Braking

Vehicle Categories & Buying Guide

The racing roster is divided into three distinct classes engineered for every stage of your progression:

  • Street & Classic Class: High stability and forgiving steering, perfect for mastering city tracks.
  • Sport & Cyber Class: Powerful twin-turbo acceleration and higher top speed for intermediate competitors.
  • Apex Hypercar Class: Championship-level supercars with roaring exhaust notes and 2x coin boosts.

Expert Tuning Advice

Garage Engineer Pro Tip:

For early-game progression, prioritize upgrading Acceleration and Braking on your active car. Snappy acceleration rapidly restores cruising speed following emergency dodges, allowing you to bank gold coins much faster to unlock the Cyber Blade or Golden Beast!
